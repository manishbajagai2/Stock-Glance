Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Stock Glance (“the app”) is a personal India-equity research desk. This page explains what information is collected when you use stock-glance.onrender.com and how it is handled.

Information we collect

  • Account data. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and basic profile identifiers through Supabase Auth so we can identify your session.
  • Holdings you save. Symbols and related holding details you add are stored in our Supabase database and are private to your account (row-level security).
  • Usage of research features. Company lookups and desk runs may be processed by our application server and third-party data sources used to assemble market information.

How we use information

We use account and holdings data only to operate the app: authenticate you, show your holdings, and provide research views you request. We do not sell personal data.

Third parties

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Google — Sign in with Google
  • Render — application hosting
  • Market data / scraping providers used to fetch public company information you request

Data retention

Account and holdings data remain until you remove holdings or ask us to delete your account data. You can sign out at any time from the app.
